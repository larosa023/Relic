# Console models on the 3D stage

Free 3D models from Sketchfab, each under its author's Creative Commons license. Relic's copies
were compressed to make them about 10x smaller (meshopt geometry compression, WebP textures at
up to 1024 px, with gltf-transform); nothing else was changed. When loading, Relic rotates,
centers and resizes them, and hides a few loose parts (the N64's controller, for example).
Model choices and orientation follow Cartucho (github.com/GabrielGoris/cartucho, MIT).
