RetroArch

Other emulators

Newer consoles run in their own emulator instead of RetroArch. Relic looks for them in the usual places, or choose the .exe.

ROM folders

Relic always shows RetroArch's playlists. Add folders to pick up games RetroArch hasn't scanned. Name subfolders after the system (SNES, Genesis, PS1…) or keep one mixed folder.

BIOS files

Some consoles need a BIOS from your own console. Point Relic at the folder you keep them in: it recognises each one and puts it where its emulator looks, now and after every new install.
